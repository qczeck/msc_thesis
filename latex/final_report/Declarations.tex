\tocsubsectionstar{Ethical Considerations}
This project used only publicly available benchmark datasets (CIFAR-100, ImageNet-100, and Horizon Lines in the Wild v2), involved no human participants or personal data, and raised no ethical concerns requiring approval.

\tocsubsectionstar{Sustainability}
The scripts were tested and designed in a way to avoid wasting GPU compute time due to misconfigured jobs.

\tocsubsectionstar{Availability of Data and Materials}
Source code is available at: \url{https://github.com/qczeck/msc_thesis} - it contains RoPART implementation, evaluation, and training scripts. The upstream PART~\cite{part} implementation is vendored unmodified under \texttt{PART/}.

Models checkpoints are not published due to their large size; available on request. 

Datasets used:
\begin{itemize}
\item CIFAR-100: auto-downloaded by \texttt{torchvision}
\item ImageNet-100: mirror downloaded from HuggingFace via \texttt{ropart/scripts/\_download\_imagenet100.py}
\item HLW v2: obtained from the authors by request, used under their terms.
\end{itemize}
Derived data can be re-derived by using appropriate scripts in the repository. Figures can also be regenerated.
