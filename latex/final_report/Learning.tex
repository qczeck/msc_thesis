\ourchapter{Does relative orientation learn?}\label{ch:learning}

\stub{Chapter preamble: this chapter asks whether the \dphi{} signal exists at all,
and whether an apparent positive is genuine relative orientation or the
interpolation artefact. It is presented chronologically because the chronology is
the argument --- each experiment was designed to close the previous one's threat to
validity.}

\section{The correctness gate}\label{sec:correctness-gate}
\stub{The translation-only baseline reproducing PART's behaviour, with translational
negative symmetry as the gate. Nothing that follows is interpretable without this.}

\section{The first negative: raw rotation at \texorpdfstring{$P=4$}{P=4}}\label{sec:first-negative}
\stub{Rotation pinned at the mean-predictor floor, and translation collapsing with
it. The single-batch overfit harness separates task from wiring: the model fits
memorisable labels, so the failure is recoverability, not a bug.}

\section{Recoverability: \texorpdfstring{$P=8$}{P=8} and the quad probes}\label{sec:recoverability}
\stub{More orientation-bearing content per patch barely moves rotation off chance.
The discrete-quad probes, which are confound-free by construction, sit at floor too.}

\section{Large patches unlock \texorpdfstring{\dphi}{the orientation channel}}\label{sec:p32-unlock}
\stub{At \(P=32\), bounded \(\pm 30^\circ\) rotation learns and passes the intrinsic
gates --- the first positive, and the point at which the confound stops being
hypothetical.}

\section{Adjudicating the interpolation confound}\label{sec:adjudication}
\stub{Blur removal collapses the positive to floor on every channel. The extraction,
statistics and gradients are all exonerated, so the collapse is read as a result:
the signature was doing the work.}

\section{The reversal: isolating optimisation drag}\label{sec:reversal}
\stub{Down-weighting the rotation group separates \emph{signal absent} from
\emph{optimisation collapsed}. With the drag controlled, blur-removed rotation
learns identically to raw --- the signature verdict does not survive, and the
positive is genuine.}

\section{What the arc establishes}\label{sec:arc-establishes}
\stub{The claim this chapter supports, stated no more strongly than the evidence
allows, and the loose end that remains: signature removal is near-total, not total.}
